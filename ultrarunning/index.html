Trail Shoes

Top Pick — Marathon

Asics Magic Speed 2

$100

Not sure you can find a better deal on a shoe w/ a carbon plate. (For a marathon) the carbon plate gives you late-race pop when your legs are gone. I ran 1st marathon in Magic Speed (Houston 4:30-jan2023). Ran 2nd marathon (Houston 4:06-jan2025) and 100k (Brazos Bend 15ish-dec2025) in Magic Speed 2. Not the best times, but my left knee would not have let me finish w/o these. Note: Huge fan of these for marathons (if you can find a deal ~$100-$120)- completely worth it and will use again. Running first 100miler (Brazos Bend 100) this year, and I am going to go in another direction - training w/ zero drop Altras.

100-Miler Race Plan

Altra Lone Peak 8

~$100–$140

Altra's zero-drop trail workhorse, and one of the two shoes I plan to swap between during the Brazos Bend 100. The other is the Altra Olympus. I'm still breaking these in and haven't finished an ultra in them yet, so consider this a first impression: so far they're comfortable and solid. I'll post a full update after the race. Fit tip: they seem to run about a half size big, so try a pair on in a store if you can. Luckily, Amazon and Altra both have solid return policies. You can also just let them run a little big, since your feet swell over long miles.

Speed Day Pick

Saucony Havok XC3

~$30

My go-to for speed workouts. Light, low to the ground, nothing extra between you and the track, and at this price it's hard to find anything better. A typical running shoe lasts 300–500 miles. I retire these at about half that, because a worn-out minimalist shoe gives you nothing back on hard efforts. That would hurt with a $150 shoe, but it's easy at $30. Fit tip: they run small, so I order a full size up from my normal running shoe.

100-Miler Training

Altra Escalante 4

~$110–$130

My step up from the Havoks. It keeps the same flat, close-to-the-ground feel, but has enough cushion to stay comfortable deep into a long run, so it's the shoe I'm putting my 100-miler training miles on. It's zero drop, meaning your heel sits level with your forefoot. That takes some getting used to if you're coming from a traditional shoe, so build up your mileage in them gradually. It's not just a training shoe, either: a buddy of mine ran the 2025 Chevron Houston Marathon in a pair. Fit tip: true to size. I wear my usual 10.5.
