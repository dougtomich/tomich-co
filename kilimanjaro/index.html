Footwear

Kili Tested

Altra Timp 5 Hiker GTX

~$190

My son and I both wore these on our Kilimanjaro climb, and neither of us would change a thing. Altra's roomy toe box left space for an extra pair of thick socks on the cold nights without cramping our toes. The Vibram sole gripped well on rock, and the mid-height collar kept our ankles steady over uneven ground, uphill and down. It's GORE-TEX waterproof and feels more like a trail shoe than a clunky boot. Fit tip: true to size. My normal 10.5 was spot on.

Layering System

Base Layer

LAPASA Men's Ultra-Heavyweight Thermal Set (M63)

~$45

This is the base layer I wore to the Uhuru summit, and it was plenty warm — summit night at Kili dropped into the -15°F range with wind chill and I never felt under-dressed up top. At 560 g/m² with a brushed fleece interior (polyester/cotton/spandex outer, poly/spandex inner), it's an ultra-heavyweight that punches well above its $45 price. Not merino, so you won't get the multi-day stink-free trick, but you can rinse it at camp and it dries overnight. Fit tip: order true to size for a snug thermal fit; size up one if you want to layer a second mid under your shell on summit night.
