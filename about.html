About Tomich LLC

Doug's spent 20 years figuring out which products are worth it and which ones are just good marketing. Gisele grew up in Brazil, where curly hair care is a way of life. They built Tomich LLC around the stuff they'd recommend to a friend — whether it made them money or not.

What We Do

Tomich LLC is a family-run brand house. We make our own products — starting with hair care rooted in the Brazilian tradition — and we share honest recommendations for gear we've actually used. No sponsored placements, no fluff.

How This Site Makes Money

Some links on this site are Amazon affiliate links. When you click one and make a purchase, we earn a small commission — usually a few cents to a few dollars. It doesn't cost you anything extra. This helps cover hosting and gives us a reason to keep the recommendations updated.
