Curl Care Rooted in the Brazilian Tradition.

Gisele grew up in Brazil, where taking care of curly hair isn't a trend — it's a way of life. Tomich Hair brings that tradition to products made for 3C curls and beyond.

The Story

In Brazil, curly hair care is passed down through generations. The techniques, the ingredients, the patience it takes — Gisele learned all of it growing up. When she couldn't find products in the U.S. that matched what she knew worked, she started making her own.

Tomich Hair is the result: curl care products built on real knowledge, not marketing trends. Starting with a 3C curl activator that does what it says — defines curls, adds moisture, and doesn't leave buildup.
