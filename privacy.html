Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Overview

Tomich.co ("we," "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect and how we use it.

Information We Collect

We collect limited information automatically when you visit: your browser type, device type, pages visited, time spent, and general location derived from IP address. We do not collect your name, email, or payment details unless you voluntarily provide them — for example, by subscribing to our newsletter.

How We Use It

Analytics data helps us understand which gear reviews are useful so we can write better ones. Newsletter emails are used only to send the updates you signed up for. Affiliate links pass a tracking ID to Amazon so we can earn a commission — it does not identify you personally.

Third-Party Services

We use Google Analytics for traffic data, Google Fonts for typography, Beehiiv for the newsletter, and Amazon Associates for product links. Each of these services has its own privacy policy governing how they handle data.

Cookies

We use cookies for analytics and to remember your preferences. You can disable cookies in your browser settings, though some site features may not work as intended.

Your Rights

You may request deletion of any personal data we hold, unsubscribe from the newsletter at any time, or contact us with privacy questions at doug@tomich.co.

Changes

We may update this policy occasionally. The "last updated" date at the top reflects the most recent revision.
